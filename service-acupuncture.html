Acupuncture 针灸

Uses needles to promote healing and relieve pain.

Service Description

Acupuncture is a clinical procedure of traditional Chinese medicine with more than 5000 years of history. According to TCM theory, qi ("chi") circulates through 12 meridians connected to the body's organs. Injury, stress, or emotional strain can block this flow — sterile, hair-thin needles placed at specific acupoints re-establish the healthy circulation of energy, promoting natural healing and relieving pain.

针灸是拥有五千多年历史的中医临床疗法。“气”沿十二经络运行，损伤、压力或情绪波动会阻滞气血；无菌毫针刺激特定穴位，可恢复气的正常运行，促进自愈、缓解疼痛。
