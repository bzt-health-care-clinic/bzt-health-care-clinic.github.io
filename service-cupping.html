Cupping 拔罐 / GuaSha 刮痧

Uses cups to improve blood flow and relieve muscle tension.

Service Description

Cupping is a traditional therapy in which cups (glass, bamboo, or silicone) are placed on the skin to create gentle suction, usually left in place for several minutes. It stimulates blood flow, relieves muscle tension, and promotes healing — and is often combined with acupuncture and herbal medicine for a stronger effect.

拔罐是将罐具（玻璃、竹、硅胶）置于皮肤上产生负压吸附，一般留置数分钟。可促进血液循环、缓解肌肉紧张、加速恢复，常与针灸、中药配合使用，效果更佳。
