1. 三伏天养生

一、顾护阳气

  • 温通背部、艾灸是夏季养阳的好方法。
  • 空调、冷饮、冰镇茶、熬夜最伤阳气；夏季暑湿伤阳最重。

二、健脾祛湿

  • 饮食调理、肢体运动、刺激足三里穴健脾祛湿。
  • 湿热内侵则见疲倦、身重、食欲不振、腹胀、反酸、腹泻。

三、合理起居

  • 晚睡早起、午间小憩；散步、游泳、太极等轻运动至微微汗出；保持心态平和。

1. Summer Wellness on Sanfu Days

1. Protecting Yang

  • Warming the back and moxibustion are excellent ways to nurture yang in summer.
  • Air conditioning, cold drinks, iced tea, and staying up late damage yang the most; summer's heat and dampness injure yang severely.

2. Strengthening the Spleen, Dispelling Dampness

  • Regulate through diet, physical movement, and stimulating Zusanli (ST36) to strengthen the spleen and dispel dampness.
  • When damp-heat invades: fatigue, heaviness, poor appetite, bloating, acid reflux, diarrhea.

3. Healthy Daily Rhythm

  • Sleep a little later, rise early, take a midday nap; gentle exercise — walking, swimming, tai chi — until a light sweat; keep a calm mind.

2. 三伏贴 —— 冬病夏治

三伏天已至，冬病夏治好时机，错过等一年！三伏贴将药物贴敷于穴位，借外界阳气补充体内阳气、驱散深伏寒邪；艾灸亦可用于过敏性皮肤问题。

适宜人群

  1. 亚健康人群：易感冒、易疲劳、睡眠差、记忆力减退。
  2. 寒湿痹证：类风湿、强直性脊柱炎、骨关节炎、颈肩腰背痛。
  3. 呼吸系统：慢性支气管炎、哮喘、过敏性鼻炎、慢性咽炎。
  4. 消化系统：慢性胃肠炎、胃肠功能紊乱、慢性腹泻、消化不良、寒性胃痛。
  5. 妇科：寒性痛经、产后身痛、月经不调、经量偏少。

2. Sanfu Herbal Patches — Treat Winter Ailments in Summer

Sanfu days are here — the best time to treat winter ailments; miss it and wait another year! Sanfu patches apply herbal medicine to acupoints, borrowing summer's yang to replenish the body's yang and expel deep-seated cold. Moxibustion can also help allergic skin conditions.

Who Can Benefit

  1. Sub-health: prone to colds, fatigue, poor sleep, declining memory.
  2. Cold-damp bi syndromes: rheumatoid arthritis, ankylosing spondylitis, osteoarthritis, neck/shoulder/back pain.
  3. Respiratory: chronic bronchitis, asthma, allergic rhinitis, chronic pharyngitis.
  4. Digestive: chronic gastroenteritis, functional GI disorders, chronic diarrhea, indigestion, cold-type stomach pain.
  5. Gynecological: cold-type menstrual pain, postpartum body aches, irregular menstruation, scanty periods.

3. 湿疹

湿疹是温哥华常见病，反复缠绵，临床以慢性湿疹多见。这是一种过敏性炎症性皮肤病，由风、湿、热之邪，加之饮食不节、嗜酒损伤脾胃所致，极易复发。西药药膏多含激素，长期使用会使皮肤变黑；中医通过内在调理——恢复脾胃功能、排出湿毒——从根本上治愈，依体质病程配合针灸，重症加内服外用中药。

发作期禁忌

  • 急性期避免热水烫洗及肥皂等刺激。
  • 忌搔抓；忌发物：辛辣、鸡鸭、牛羊肉、海鲜鱼类，及香菜、韭菜、芹菜、生姜、葱、蒜。

3. Eczema

Eczema is common in Vancouver — lingering, recurrent, mostly chronic in clinic. It is an allergic inflammatory skin condition caused by wind, dampness, and heat pathogens, aggravated by irregular diet and alcohol damaging the spleen and stomach; it relapses easily. Western ointments often contain steroids, which darken the skin with long-term use. TCM heals it at the root — restoring spleen-stomach function and draining damp-toxins from within — with acupuncture tailored to constitution and stage; severe cases add internal and topical herbal medicine.

What to Avoid During Flares

  • During acute flares, avoid scalding hot water and irritants such as soap.
  • Don't scratch; avoid flare-triggering foods: spicy food, chicken/duck, beef/lamb, seafood and fish, plus cilantro, chives, celery, ginger, scallions, garlic.

4. 膝痛的针灸治疗及预防

膝痛多由运动损伤及年老体衰引起。现代医学常见：退行性膝骨关节炎、滑膜/韧带/半月板损伤；中医归为“痹证”，关键在“通则不痛，不通则痛”。针灸活血通络，改善局部循环、缓解疼痛、维持关节稳定、延缓软骨退化。

常见病因

  1. 外伤劳损，气滞血瘀；
  2. 卫气不固，风寒湿邪侵袭；
  3. 年老体衰，气血肝肾亏虚；
  4. 类风湿性关节炎。

4. Acupuncture for Knee Pain — Treatment & Prevention

Knee pain mostly comes from sports injuries and age-related degeneration. Common Western diagnoses: degenerative knee osteoarthritis, synovial / ligament / meniscus injuries. In TCM this is "bi syndrome" — the key principle being "where there is free flow, there is no pain; pain comes from blockage." Acupuncture moves blood and opens the channels, improving local circulation, relieving pain, maintaining joint stability, and slowing cartilage degeneration.

Common Causes

  1. Traumatic strain — qi stagnation and blood stasis;
  2. Weak defensive qi letting wind, cold, and dampness invade;
  3. Aging — deficiency of qi, blood, liver, and kidney;
  4. Rheumatoid arthritis.

5. 针灸治疗多囊卵巢综合征（PCOS）

早一天治疗，防范于未然。多囊卵巢综合征是生殖功能障碍与代谢异常并存的内分泌疾病，症状包括痤疮、多毛、雄激素性脱发、肥胖、月经稀发、月经不调、闭经、不孕。育龄女性患病率 6%–25%，肥胖者中达 41.3%，占无排卵性不孕的 50%–70%。

病因

  • 西医：遗传 + 环境（过食、压力抑郁），下丘脑-垂体-卵巢轴功能失调。
  • 中医：肾虚为本，兼肝肾心失调，痰湿、气滞、血瘀为标。

针灸分期治疗（3 个月经周期为一疗程）

  1. 经期——通；
  2. 卵泡期——补；
  3. 排卵期——疏通；
  4. 黄体期——调养并施。

5. Acupuncture for PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome)

Treat a day earlier — prevent before it happens. PCOS is an endocrine disorder combining reproductive dysfunction and metabolic abnormality. Symptoms include acne, hirsutism, androgenic hair loss, obesity, infrequent or irregular menstruation, amenorrhea, and infertility. It affects 6%–25% of women of reproductive age — up to 41.3% among those with obesity — and accounts for 50%–70% of anovulatory infertility.

Causes

  • Western medicine: genetics + environment (overeating, stress/depression), dysfunction of the hypothalamic-pituitary-ovarian axis.
  • TCM: kidney deficiency as the root, with liver-kidney-heart disharmony; phlegm-dampness, qi stagnation, and blood stasis as the manifestations.

Phased Acupuncture Treatment (one course = 3 menstrual cycles)

  1. Menstrual phase — move;
  2. Follicular phase — tonify;
  3. Ovulatory phase — dredge;
  4. Luteal phase — regulate and nourish.

6. 针灸催产

针灸可促进子宫收缩、缓解疼痛、缩短产程；治疗后 3 分钟至 8 小时内分娩（因体质而异）。

五大好处

  1. 对母婴无副作用；
  2. 缩短产程；
  3. 宫缩更强、更有节律；
  4. 镇静止痛；
  5. 减少产后出血。

最佳时机：胎膜破裂或宫缩开始后，以及第二产程（宫口开全至胎儿娩出）。

6. Induce Labour with Acupuncture

Acupuncture can promote uterine contractions, relieve pain, and shorten labour; delivery typically follows within 3 minutes to 8 hours after treatment (varies by constitution).

Five Benefits

  1. No side effects for mother or baby;
  2. Shorter labour;
  3. Stronger, more rhythmic contractions;
  4. Calming and pain relief;
  5. Less postpartum bleeding.

Best timing: after membranes rupture or contractions begin, and during the second stage of labour (from full dilation to delivery).

7. 针灸转胎

怀孕 7–8 个月发现胎位异常（如臀位）时，艾灸可帮助转胎，实现顺产。

7. Moxibustion for Breech Position

If abnormal fetal position (breech) is found at 7–8 months of pregnancy, moxibustion can help turn the fetus to enable a normal delivery.
