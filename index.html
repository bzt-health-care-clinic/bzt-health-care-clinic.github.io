FAQ 常见问题

Acupuncture is a clinical procedure of traditional Chinese medicine with 5000+ years of history. Qi ("chi") circulates through 12 meridians linked to the organs; injury, stress, or strong emotions can block this flow. Sterile needles placed at specific points re-establish the healthy flow of energy.

针灸是拥有五千多年历史的中医临床疗法。“气”沿十二经络运行，损伤、压力或情绪波动会阻滞气血运行，无菌针具刺激特定穴位可恢复气的正常运行。

  1. Only licensed / registered acupuncturists should perform treatment — our practitioner is fully registered.
  2. We use single-use sterile disposable needles (one needle per person, discarded after use).
  3. Minor side effects are possible: slight bleeding, a small bruise, or brief soreness/numbness.
  4. Pregnant patients, or people with bleeding disorders / coagulation dysfunction, should consult a doctor first.

请认准注册针灸师；本诊所使用一次性无菌针具（一人一针，用后即弃）；偶有轻微出血、瘀青或短暂酸麻；孕妇及凝血功能障碍者请先咨询医生。

  • Wear loose, comfortable clothing.
  • Have a small meal 1–2 hours before — don't come on an empty stomach, overeat, or drink alcohol beforehand.
  • Bring relevant medical reports / test results.
  • Arrive 5–10 minutes early; please turn off cell phones during treatment.

穿宽松衣物；治疗前 1–2 小时少量进食（勿空腹、过饱或饮酒）；携带相关检查报告；提前 5–10 分钟到达；治疗期间请关闭手机。本诊所采用专业消毒措施。

Acupuncture is essentially painless. You may feel a slight tingling, tugging, or soreness on insertion that passes quickly. A heavy, sore, or tingling sensation during treatment ("deqi") is normal — most people feel deep comfort and relaxation afterward.

针灸基本无痛，进针时或有轻微酸麻胀感、转瞬即逝；治疗中的酸胀麻属正常“得气”反应，治疗后多感身心放松舒适。
