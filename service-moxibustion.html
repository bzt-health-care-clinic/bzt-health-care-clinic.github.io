Ear Seeds 贴耳豆

Attach ear seeds to acupoints to treat illnesses.

Service Description

Ear acupuncture using "ear seeds" — small, dark, round medicinal-herb balls (Wang Bu Liu Xing / 王不留行, about the size of a millet grain) taped onto ear acupoints with medical adhesive. Gentle, non-invasive, and effective for a wide range of conditions.

贴耳豆属耳针疗法：将王不留行籽（小米粒大小、灰黑色药丸）以医用胶布贴压于耳部穴位，温和无创，可调理多种病症。
